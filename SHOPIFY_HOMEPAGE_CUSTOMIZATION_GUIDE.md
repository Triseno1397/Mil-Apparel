# Shopify Homepage Customization Guide
## Making Mil Apparel Look Professional & Ready to Launch

Follow these steps in your Shopify theme editor (the page you currently have open).

---

## Step 1: Update Hero Section

**Current:** "Browse our latest products"  
**Goal:** Military-focused, professional messaging

### In the Shopify Editor:

1. **Click on the hero image section** (the top banner with the illustration)
2. **Change the heading text** to one of these options:
   - "Premium Military Apparel for Those Who Serve"
   - "Honor Your Service with Premium Mil Apparel"
   - "Military Pride. American Made."
3. **Update the button text** from "Shop all" to:
   - "Shop By Branch" or "Explore Collections"
4. **Add a subheading** (if there's an option):
   - "Supporting veterans and active duty with pride"

### Hero Image:
- Consider replacing the current illustration with:
  - A photo of someone wearing military apparel
  - An American flag background
  - Military-themed imagery
  - **For now:** The current illustration works, but note this for future improvement

---

## Step 2: Add Featured Collections Section

### In the Left Sidebar:

1. **Click "Add section"** (below the hero)
2. **Select "Featured collection"**
3. **Configure it:**
   - Title: "Shop By Branch"
   - Create or select collections: Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Coast Guard
4. **Click "Add section"** again
5. **Add another "Featured collection"**:
   - Title: "Best Sellers" or "Popular Items"

---

## Step 3: Add Trust/Credibility Section

### Add Credibility Badges:

1. **Click "Add section"**
2. **Select "Rich text" or "Image with text"**
3. **Add content:**
   ```
   🇺🇸 Veteran-Owned Business
   ✅ 100% Satisfaction Guaranteed
   🚚 Fast & Free Shipping
   💪 Supporting Those Who Serve
   ```

**Alternative:** Add an "Image banner" section with:
- Title: "Proudly Veteran-Owned"
- Text: "Every purchase supports veterans and active military families"

---

## Step 4: Improve Product Display

The "Products" section looks good, but let's enhance it:

1. **Click on the "Products" section**
2. **Change settings:**
   - Title: "Featured Products" or "New Arrivals"
   - Show 4-8 products
   - Enable "View all" link

---

## Step 5: Update Header/Logo

### In the Header Section:

1. **Click "Header"** in the left sidebar
2. **Add a logo:**
   - If you don't have one yet, you can use text: "MIL APPAREL"
   - Font: Bold, clean, military-style
3. **Navigation Menu:**
   - Home
   - Shop by Branch dropdown (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Coast Guard)
   - About Us
   - Contact

---

## Step 6: Footer Polish

### Click "Footer" section:

Add these elements:

**Column 1 - About:**
- "About Mil Apparel"
- "Veteran-owned and operated, providing premium military apparel..."

**Column 2 - Quick Links:**
- Shipping & Returns
- Size Guide
- Contact Us
- FAQs

**Column 3 - Contact:**
- Email address
- Social media links (if applicable)

**Column 4 - Newsletter:**
- "Sign up for exclusive offers"
- Email signup form

---

## Step 7: Color Scheme (Professional Touch)

### In Theme Settings:

1. **Click the paintbrush icon** or "Theme settings"
2. **Colors:**
   - **Primary:** Navy Blue or Olive Green
   - **Accent:** Red or Gold
   - **Background:** White or Light Gray
   - **Buttons:** Bold military colors (Navy, Army Green, Red)

---

## Step 8: Add Announcement Bar

### At the very top:

1. **Enable "Announcement bar"** in theme sections
2. **Text options:**
   - "🇺🇸 Free Shipping on Orders Over $50"
   - "Proudly Veteran-Owned | Supporting Those Who Serve"
   - "10% Off Your First Order - Use Code: MILSTRONG"

---

## Quick Wins Checklist

Before you publish, make sure:

- [ ] Hero section has clear, military-focused messaging
- [ ] At least 2-3 product sections are visible
- [ ] Collections are organized by branch
- [ ] Veteran-owned messaging is prominent
- [ ] Navigation menu is clear and organized
- [ ] Footer has contact info and policies
- [ ] Announcement bar is enabled with an offer
- [ ] Mobile preview looks good (use mobile icon in editor)

---

## After These Changes

Your homepage will have:

✅ Professional hero section with clear value proposition  
✅ Organized product collections by military branch  
✅ Trust signals (veteran-owned, satisfaction guaranteed)  
✅ Clear navigation and footer  
✅ Consistent military/patriotic branding  
✅ Ready to go live!
