# Mill Apparel - Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

**Name**: Mill Apparel  
**Category**: Premium military identity + lifestyle apparel store  
**Positioning**: Clean, modern, premium (not cheesy/meme-y)

## Core Products

- T-shirts
- Hoodies
- Caps
- Patches
- Mugs
- Wallets

## Product Types

1. **Preset Designs**: Ready-to-buy items organized by Branch + Role/Specialty
2. **Customizable Products**: Guided customization flow with personalization options
   - Unit text
   - Name
   - Number
   - Dates
   - Color options

## Target Audience

### Primary
- Active-duty military personnel
- Veterans
- Military families

### Secondary
- Supporters of the military
- Gift buyers for military occasions

### Key Occasions
- PCS (Permanent Change of Station)
- Promotions
- Retirements
- Birthdays
- Holidays

## Key Differentiators

1. **Clean Premium Aesthetic**: Modern, refined design (NOT cheesy or meme-heavy)
2. **Intuitive Organization**: Shop by Branch + Shop by Role/Specialty
3. **Simple Customization**: High-converting wizard with guided flow, live preview, clear pricing
4. **Gift-Friendly**: Curated collections, bundles, upsells (patch + mug + tee sets)
5. **Trust & Quality**: Durable blanks, great fit, fast support

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Characteristics
- **Premium**: Refined, quality-focused
- **Respectful**: Honors military service authentically
- **Modern**: Contemporary, not dated or cliché
- **Clear**: Straightforward, no fluff

### Writing Guidelines
- Use clean, concise copy
- Respect military culture without being heavy-handed
- Avoid unverified claims (e.g., "veteran-owned") unless confirmed
- Focus on quality, durability, and fit
- Make gift-giving easy and obvious

### Example Copy Tone
✅ "Premium apparel for those who serve"  
✅ "Built to last, designed to honor"  
✅ "Your service, your style"  
❌ "AMERICA! FREEDOM! EAGLES!!!"  
❌ "Only REAL veterans understand"  
❌ "Tactical operator gear for warriors"

## Trust Signals

- Quality materials (durable blanks)
- Great fit guarantee
- Fast customer support
- Clear return policy
- Secure checkout
- Real product photography (when available)

## Competitive Positioning

We are **NOT**:
- Cheesy military meme apparel
- Tactical/operator gear store
- Discount/budget military merch
- Political statement brand

We **ARE**:
- Premium lifestyle brand for military community
- Modern, clean aesthetic
- Quality-focused with smart organization
- Gift-friendly and conversion-optimized
